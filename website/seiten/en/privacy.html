Privacy

As of 30 October 2026

In short

  • This website sets no cookies and measures nothing — no analytics, no advertising, no forms.
  • Fonts, images and script are served from the same server. Reading the site loads nothing from third parties.

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When a page is requested, GitHub processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, requested address, referrer and browser information. This serves the delivery and security of the website. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

Links to GitHub

Source code, releases and the installer are hosted on GitHub. When you follow these links or download a file, GitHub's privacy statement applies there.

The phpdbadmin software

phpdbadmin runs on your own server. We receive no data from it — there is no telemetry and nothing phones home. During setup, the web installer downloads the latest release from GitHub. The SQL help with AI is off until your installation switches it on; then the question and the schema go to Anthropic with your own key, never data from your tables.

Your rights

You have the right to information about the data we process about you, and to correction and deletion — under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). As this website itself stores no personal data, such requests usually concern only the hosting provider's server logs. You may lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
